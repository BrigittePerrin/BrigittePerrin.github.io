A little background, a lot of perspective

Hiya, I'm Brigitte.

I'm a service & experience designer and researcher interested in the connections between people, technology, and the systems shaping our everyday lives.

An unconventional path into design

Before focusing on experience and service design, I worked across software engineering, quality, product and project work, and consulting. That background continues to shape how I approach a design problem: I care about the human experience, but I also want to understand the technical and organizational reality behind it.

My graduate work in Human Factors in Information Design has helped me put research and human behavior at the center of that perspective. As I deepen my experience at Elevations Credit Union, I'm continuing to explore how research can guide better services, more inclusive experiences, and smarter decisions.

I like the questions that don't fit neatly inside a single interface: What happens before and after an interaction? Who else is affected by a design choice? What should we measure beyond speed and ease?

For me, good design starts with understanding people — and keeps going until we understand the system around them.
