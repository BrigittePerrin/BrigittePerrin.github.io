Designing for operators under pressure

What does usability look like when someone is wearing gloves, managing cognitive load, and making high-stakes decisions?

ReySecur / MailSecur interface evaluation — A closer look at the problem, the research that shaped the response, and the role I played in moving the work forward.

My role
UX Researcher & Project Lead
Context
Security technology · Enterprise equipment
Duration
3 months

01 / The challenge

MailSecur is a mail-screening system used in environments where accuracy and clarity matter. The team assessed the existing interface and proposed improvements based on usability heuristics, operational context, and competitive research.

02 / What research revealed

  • Ambiguous icons and inconsistent controls created avoidable interpretation work.
  • Limited system feedback made interactions less predictable.
  • Target size, contrast, and layout needed to accommodate real operating conditions, including glove use.
The important question wasn't just “does it work?” — it was “does it work for the people using it, in their real context?”

03 / My contribution

My contribution centered on research, evaluation, project planning, and design strategy. I led heuristic and competitor analysis, synthesized recommendations, and supported the design team with evidence.

The final visual UI redesign was executed by teammates using the team’s shared findings. My contribution was to the research and the direction that informed those designs.

04 / The outcome

The team delivered prioritized interface recommendations and a proposed redesign aligned with human factors and the client’s constraints. No deployment or quantified performance improvements are claimed.
