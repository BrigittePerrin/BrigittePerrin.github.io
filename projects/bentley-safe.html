Making campus safety easier to navigate

A safety app can contain the right tools and still make them difficult to find when someone needs them most.

BentleySafe / Campus safety mobile app — A closer look at the problem, the research that shaped the response, and the role I played in moving the work forward.

My role
UX/UI Researcher, Designer & Project Lead
Context
Higher education · Mobile safety
Duration
3 months

01 / The challenge

BentleySafe is a campus safety application built on the AppArmor white-label platform. The challenge was to clarify high-priority actions and improve navigation without ignoring the constraints of the underlying system.

Our team evaluated the existing experience through a campus survey, comparison with other safety apps, and heuristic review.

02 / What research revealed

  • Critical safety features weren’t visually separated from lower-priority tools.
  • Feature names and navigation did not consistently match user mental models.
  • Emergency actions were spread across different areas of the interface.
In an emergency, finding the right action quickly is part of feeling safe.

03 / My contribution

I served as UX/UI researcher, designer, and project lead on a four-person team. I guided research and synthesis, prioritized features, and translated insights into information architecture and screen designs.

Design decisions included clearer labels, a stronger hierarchy for urgent actions, centralizing emergency contacts, and grouping supporting features under “My Safety Toolbox.”

04 / The outcome

The project produced research-led recommendations and a redesigned interface grounded in emergency usability and platform feasibility. This is a design project; implementation or real-world performance outcomes are not claimed.
